Chungas World

A Minecraft Bedrock world of custom roller coasters and real-place recreations, with parks, restaurants, a school and college, a games hub and more to explore. Join the Realm and ride.

Request access Join our Discord

How to join

  1. Check you have Minecraft Bedrock Edition. You need a Bedrock account to join.
  2. Fill in the request form on the world access page.
  3. Wait for approval. The joining info is sent to you privately, so there is no public server IP.
  4. Join the Realm and head for a park.

Chungas World runs on a Bedrock Realm, which allows up to 10 players at a time. The server is open 24 hours a day while it’s online. Access requests aren’t answered from 1 July to 31 August. Check server outages if you can’t get in.

Quick answers

Do I need a Minecraft account?

Yes. You need a Bedrock Edition account to join.

Is there voice chat?

Not in game, but players can chat on Discord if they choose. See the Discord access info.

When can I play?

The server is open 24 hours a day while it’s online. Staff are on the server from 06:00 to 23:00 every day.

What changes in summer?

From 1 July to 31 August, access requests aren’t answered, and some areas may be unavailable for seasonal updates.

Is it safe for kids?

We try to keep the server safe for all ages, with moderated chats and monitored gameplay. We can’t guarantee complete safety, so younger players should join with parental guidance.

Can my club or business move in?

Yes. The “Move Your Org Into Chungas World” programme gives groups space for an in-world presence. Contact the team to start.
